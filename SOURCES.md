# 評分設計參考（不含權重與公式）

- American Academy of Sleep Medicine / Sleep Research Society: healthy adults should regularly obtain 7 or more hours of sleep.
- World Health Organization: adults should accumulate 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week (or equivalent), with muscle-strengthening activity on 2 or more days per week; any activity is better than none.
- National Academies: water needs vary substantially with environment and activity; total water includes beverages and food, so the app avoids treating one exact water number as a universal requirement.
- WHO / CDC: frequent sugar-sweetened beverage intake is associated with adverse health outcomes; free sugars should be limited.
- Gaming is treated as a balance/displacement variable rather than “gaming is inherently bad”: the internal penalty is more sensitive when long gaming coincides with reduced sleep, activity, or priority work.

The score is a behavior-support heuristic, not a medical or nutritional diagnosis.
